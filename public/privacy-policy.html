Raffia LEGACY PROJECT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The Raffia Legacy Project (“Raffia Legacy”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is collected and used when you visit our website, contact us, submit an enquiry or form, place an order, or subscribe to our updates.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, telephone number, organisation and the information you include in a message or enquiry.
  • Order and delivery details: products, quantities, order references, delivery address, city, state, country and order notes.
  • Payment and transaction details: payment status, amount, transaction reference and information needed to reconcile an order. Our current marketplace checkout uses manual payment arrangements confirmed with our team. Do not send full card numbers, PINs, passwords or one-time passcodes through our forms or messages.
  • Newsletter details: your email address and your subscription preference or consent.
  • Technical and usage information: device and browser information, approximate location derived from network information, pages visited, referral information and interactions with the website. Cookies or similar technologies may be used for functionality and analytics.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries; administer partnerships, programmes and event requests; create and fulfil marketplace orders; coordinate delivery and payment confirmation; send newsletter updates where you have opted in; maintain and improve the website; protect against fraud and misuse; and meet legal or regulatory obligations.

3. Orders and payments

When you place a marketplace order, the details you submit are used to create and administer that order. Our current checkout flow records the order and may open WhatsApp so you can coordinate payment and fulfilment with the Raffia Legacy team. Payment is handled through the manual method confirmed with you. We do not ask you to enter or store payment-card PINs, passwords or one-time passcodes on this website.

Keep your bank and payment credentials private. Any bank or payment service you use to complete a transfer will handle your payment information under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Forms and communications

Information submitted through enquiry, collaboration, programme, event or other forms is used to review and respond to your request and, where relevant, to maintain related records. Please submit only information that is relevant to your enquiry and that you are authorised to share.

5. Newsletter

If you subscribe to the Raffia Legacy Letter, we use your email address to send occasional project, cultural, programme and opportunity updates. You may unsubscribe using the method provided in an email or contact us through the website to request removal from the mailing list.

6. Cookies and analytics

We may use essential storage technologies to support website functions and analytics tools to understand traffic and improve our services. These tools may process technical and usage information. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling some technologies may affect website functionality.

7. When information is shared

We may share relevant information with service providers that help us host and operate the website, store records, process communications, measure website usage, coordinate orders or provide other services. We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect users or our rights, or in connection with a lawful business or organisational change. We do not sell personal information.

8. Storage, security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including order administration, recordkeeping and legal compliance.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, object to or request limits on certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain information where the law requires or permits it.

10. Third-party websites and services

Our website may link to third-party websites or services, including messaging, payment and social platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Please review those policies before providing information to them.

11. Children

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorisation. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us through the website so we can review the request.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the enquiry options on our Raffia Legacy website and include “Privacy Request” in your message.
